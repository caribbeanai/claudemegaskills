# Task 1 · The market shuttle

Copy the text inside the block. Keep the sources and assignment identical in every trial.

~~~text
A town council must decide whether to fund a Saturday shuttle to its market. Write a memo of at most 120 words for the council. Recommend a next step and explain what the records support. Use only the records below and cite their bracketed IDs.

[A] Market office ledger. On the last two regular Saturdays before the trial: 182 and 194 market entries. On the two trial Saturdays: 252 and 178 entries. The ledger counts entries, including repeat visits. The first trial Saturday coincided with a music festival. The second had heavy rain.

[B] Operator's note. The shuttle recorded 110 one-way boardings in total over the two trial Saturdays. It did not track unique riders, destinations after boarding or what riders would have done without the shuttle.

[C] Market newsletter. It says 110 people visited the market because of the shuttle. The editor says the number came from the operator's note [B].

[D] Treasurer's email. The council can pay for up to two additional trial Saturdays before its budget vote. A full-season quote is pending.
~~~
