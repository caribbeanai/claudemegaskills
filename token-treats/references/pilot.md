# First pilot · Market shuttle

Four isolated Codex runs received the same market-shuttle task. Each run received the add-ons assigned to its version. A separate Codex reviewer scored the anonymised memos using the six checks before seeing the version labels. This was an exploratory test with one run per version and one task. The reviewer was a model. Human award confirmation remains pending.

| Version | Decision | Context | Units | Source dependence | Evidence | Delivery | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A · Plain | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 5/6 |
| B · Treat promise | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6/6 |
| C · Checklist | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6/6 |
| D · Both | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 6/6 |

The plain memo linked each count to weather or a festival, then left the reader to infer how those conditions complicate attribution. The reviewer withheld the context point because the link was implicit. A scorer who accepts that wording could give all four memos 6/6. The treat promise added a point to the plain prompt in this scoring and zero points when the checklist was already present. B, C and D reached the ceiling. The pilot did not test a post-answer award or measure results on the other platforms.

### Version B

> Fund two more trial Saturdays and get the full-season quote before the budget vote [D].
>
> The market logged 182 and 194 entries on regular Saturdays, 252 during the festival and 178 in heavy rain. The ledger includes repeat visits, and the different conditions make it hard to isolate the shuttle’s effect [A]. The operator counted 110 one-way boardings. The newsletter used that same figure to claim 110 additional visitors, which the operator’s records cannot establish [B][C].
>
> During the next trial, count unique riders and ask whether they’re going to the market and would have gone without the shuttle. Use those responses to estimate additional visits and weigh them against the season cost.

### Version C

>

>
> Choose comparable Saturdays. Count unique market-bound riders and ask whether they would have visited without the shuttle. Use the estimated additional visits and the full-season quote to decide whether to fund the service.
